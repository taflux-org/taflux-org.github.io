taflux

A knowledge base for people and AI in which every tile in the knowledge mosaic shows whether it still applies and who stands behind it. AI helps – but we still have the final say.

taflux on GitHub from 30 Nov 2026 Get involved

Why

AI adoption makes knowledge management essential – and affordable.

Company knowledge ages – often all by itself. Thanks to our tacit knowledge, we humans have always coped well with contradictions, inaccuracies and outdated data. Invalid knowledge is annoying, but not a real problem.

As soon as AI agents work with it, however, it becomes a risk. That is why taflux doesn’t simply deliver whatever is written somewhere, but what applies today – with a date, a level of bindingness and a clear statement of who stands behind it.

AI prepares, but people decide. AI makes maintaining information affordable – but it doesn’t make us redundant.

Principles

  • Staying current is a choice

    Knowledge goes out of date even when nobody is working on it. That is why it needs confirming (periodically and whenever contradictions arise), and new knowledge puts the older knowledge it affects up for review. AI detects and suggests – people decide.

  • Validity and authority stay with people

    Whether something applies is always confirmed by a person, and every confirmation is logged. AI prepares what applies – but the decision stays with people (human in the loop).

  • Knowledge with edges

    Knowledge blocks don’t exist in isolation: they are linked to one another by logical relations such as “superseded by” or “exception to”. AI suggests such links, which people review and approve. That way, every change shows what else ought to be reviewed.

  • Visible gaps

    If confidential, unreviewed or outdated data is missing, the answer makes that gap visible instead of hiding it or filling it with guesswork.

  • Protection through absence

    Whatever an AI system is not allowed to see simply does not exist in its search index. The company policies held in taflux determine in advance, for each system, which data is indexed. Because unapproved knowledge is physically absent from the index, sensitive data is protected against unwanted leakage even if a system is breached.

  • Version history creates traceability

    Existing documents stay where they are. taflux doesn’t replace your cloud storage. It keeps a record alongside it of what currently applies.

Six dimensions

Every knowledge block – each tile in the mosaic – carries its own state across six dimensions that are set independently of one another. The same mosaic therefore looks quite different depending on the dimension. Choose a dimension:

Validity

Does this still apply, or has it been superseded – and if so, by what?

In March the new expenses policy comes out. The old version isn’t deleted. It fades – and points to the new one that replaces it. Anyone asking about expenses gets the new version. The old one remains traceable.

Trust level

Has a person confirmed exactly this text? taflux derives this from the confirmations. If the text changes, it loses its confirmation.

Lea from Accounts confirms the section on hotel costs – it lights up. Whatever the AI captured yesterday stays an outline until someone takes a look. And where a confirmation has grown too old, it fades again: the review is due.

Bindingness

Is it a binding requirement, a recommendation or information? This determines the ranking and the tone of the answer.

The cap on hotel costs is a requirement, the tip about a cheap train connection a recommendation, the list of partner hotels information. The AI answers accordingly: a requirement comes across as a rule, not as well-meant advice.

Confidentiality

Who can access this information, and which AI systems are allowed to use it? A Taflux policy maps each trust tier to the relevant AI search indexes.

Marco asks a cloud assistant about the project costs. It may never see the cost calculations – they simply aren’t in its search index. So the answer points out that six tiles are deliberately missing. For the complete answer, Marco switches to the local model.

Retention purpose

Why keep something that no longer applies? As a lesson, as part of history or as a reference.

The four superseded tiles from “Validity” haven’t disappeared. The failed migration of 2021 stays as a lesson, the old organisation chart as history, the former standard as a reference. The AI presents none of them as a rule in force.

Autonomy level

May the AI change the stored knowledge or its metadata? Only on request, within a defined scope or on its own.

The AI fixes a typo in the style guide itself, adjusts wording in the handbook within the agreed scope and only makes suggestions for the expenses policy. How much freedom it gets is decided by a person – and it can never confirm anything.

One file

AI models come and go but the knowledge stays. Switch providers, and you simply take your knowledge base with you.

Everything is stored as plain Markdown in Git, in the Open Knowledge Format (OKF). What taflux knows about a knowledge block sits in the file’s YAML header – one line per knowledge block. Every change stays readable in the diff, and a simple grep finds it. On export, nothing is translated. Whatever must not leave is simply left out. 

type: guideline
title: Expenses policy
status: draft
taflux:
  profile: taflux-okf/1
  sections:
    - { id: sec-b1e8d4, validity: current,
        tier: binding, classification: internal, … }
    - { id: sec-c2f9a1, validity: superseded,
        superseded_by: sec-d4e117, … }

What taflux is made of

  • Format

    Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.2

    Every Markdown file is a complete, valid OKF file – no sidecar file and no conversion on export. Whatever OKF doesn’t cover, taflux adds under its own “taflux” key.

  • Editor

    Extended OKF editor built on Milkdown

    Markdown for the text, the six dimensions as controls, plus a review view and three-way merge – so anyone can contribute, even without Git. In German, French, Italian and English.

  • MCP server

    Model Context Protocol

    Connects taflux to any AI system that speaks MCP. Searches by keyword and by meaning at the same time, knows the links between knowledge blocks and keeps a separate search index for each trust tier.

  • Team

    Together, with clear responsibilities

    Anyone on the team who files or confirms something vouches for it by name – and taflux remembers who stood behind what, and when. Who may read and change what can be narrowed down: to the project team, a department or the executive board.

    • Confidentiality routing: one search index per trust tier
    • OKF-based: import, derived trust level, bundle export

    • Bundles with audience, addressing and inbox

    • OKF-native: everything in one file, derived trust level, export as a filter
  3. 30 November 2026

    Release as open source on GitHub at github.com/taflux-org.

  4. After that
    Machine maintenance
    The AI prepares due reviews and takes care of small changes – as a suggestion or directly, depending on how much freedom it has been granted beforehand. But it cannot confirm anything: whatever it changes only applies again once a person has signed it off.
    Evidence and measurement
    Audit reports, tamper-proof receipts and a reproducible benchmark for search.
    Onboarding existing content
    Take over existing documents and break them down into knowledge blocks, with safeguards for personal data.
    Fact triples
    Contradictions are no longer merely suspected but detected by rules – including when merging changes.
    Source Watcher
    When a source document changes, taflux puts the affected knowledge blocks up for review.
